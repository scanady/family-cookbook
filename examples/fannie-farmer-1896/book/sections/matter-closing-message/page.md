Every recipe in this sampler is transcribed from the 1896 first edition of *The Boston Cooking-School Cook Book*, now in the public domain. Each entry keeps Farmer's words as she printed them, with a page reference and a link to the scanned original. The photographs are new, generated for this example; none of them comes from her book.

Farmer closed her preface with a hope for the book:

"It is my wish that it may not only be looked upon as a compilation of tried and tested recipes, but that it may awaken an interest through its condensed scientific knowledge which will lead to deeper thought and broader study of what to eat."

*F. M. F.*
