# Indian Pudding

*Fannie Merritt Farmer*

*The Boston Cooking-School Cook Book* (Boston: Little, Brown, and Company, 1896), page 328. Scan of the first edition: <https://archive.org/details/bostoncookingsc00collgoog/page/n371/mode/1up>

## Original Recipe

```text
Indian Pudding.

5 cups scalded milk.               ½ cup molasses.
⅓ cup Indian meal.                 1 teaspoon salt.
            1 teaspoon ginger.

Pour milk slowly on meal, cook in double boiler twenty
minutes, add molasses, salt, and ginger; pour into but-
tered pudding-dish and bake two hours in slow oven;
serve with cream. If baked too rapidly it will not whey.
Ginger may be omitted.
```
