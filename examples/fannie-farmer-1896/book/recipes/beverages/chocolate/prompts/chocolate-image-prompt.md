# Chocolate

*Fannie Merritt Farmer*

- **Category:** Beverages
- **Cuisine:** New England
- **Yield:** Not specified
- **Prep time:** Not specified
- **Cook time:** 1 minute
- **Temperature:** Not specified

## Ingredients

- 1 1/2 squares Baker's chocolate
- 4 tablespoons sugar
- Few grains salt
- 1 cup boiling water
- 3 cups milk

## Directions

1. Scald milk.
2. Melt chocolate in small saucepan placed over hot water, add sugar, salt, and gradually boiling water.
3. When smooth, place on range and boil one minute.
4. Add to scalded milk, mill, and serve in chocolate cups with whipped cream.

## Notes

- One and one-half ounces vanilla chocolate may be substituted for Baker's chocolate; being sweetened, less sugar is required.
- To mill is Farmer's word, from the Reception Cocoa on the same page: beat two minutes, using Dover egg-beater, when froth will form, preventing scum.
