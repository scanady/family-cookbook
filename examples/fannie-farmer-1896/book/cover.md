# Back Cover

Ten recipes and two kitchen lessons from Fannie Merritt Farmer's 1896 cook book, the book that taught America to measure level. Pop-overs and brown bread, fish chowder and baked beans, a roast turkey with chestnut stuffing, and Indian pudding to finish, each in Farmer's own words beside a new photograph.
